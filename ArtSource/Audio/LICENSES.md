## Edits and implementation

See manifest.json for exact source filenames, selected time ranges, durations and output hashes. Converted to mono PCM 16-bit WAV, peaks capped at 0.65, brief fades applied to one-shots and 250 ms crossfades to ambience loops. Spray uses the creator's public HQ MP3 preview, not the login-only original WAV. Source masters are retained locally in Summative/AudioReview-2026-09-30.

Wind and river: quiet gameplay ambience (water proximity only). Grass, dirt/sand, stone and wood: local grounded ranger footsteps; landscape collision materials choose the surface. River proximity uses nearby landscape water samples. UI_Click: button presses only. Confirm/Miss: restrained restraint feedback; Confirm also for delivered arrests and purchased upgrades. Equipment: refill and spare magazine. Spray: successful pepper spray uses. Radio: tutorial radio stage only. No music, animal vocals or poacher voices were supplied by these sources.
